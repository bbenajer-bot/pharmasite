# Project Scope

**Client:** Illinois pharmacy owned by a physician (name TBD)
**Fee:** $2,000 (fixed)
**Running costs:** domain only (~$10–12/year). All services use free tiers.

## Included
- Custom-designed, mobile-friendly website with these pages: Home, Shop, Product detail, Cart, Checkout,
  Services, Prescriptions, Blog, About, Contact, FAQ, Privacy, Terms, Accessibility, Notice of Privacy Practices
- Admin dashboard (`/admin`) where the client can add or edit products, blog posts, and images;
  manage orders; and review prescription requests
- Online shop for **over-the-counter products only**, with pay-by-email ordering:
  the customer chooses a preferred payment method, and the admin sends payment instructions
- Automatic emails: new order (admin), order received / payment instructions / shipped (customer),
  new prescription request (doctor, with no health details in the email)
- Prescription request form and refill request form
- Logo (text + icon), brand colors, all site copy, 3–5 starter blog posts
- Basic SEO, Google Business Profile setup, accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA target)
- Deployment, domain connection, business email setup
- Handoff video and 2 rounds of revisions

## Not included
- Online card processing / payment gateway integration
- Custom booking system (links out to the client's existing system or Doxy.me)
- Professional photography (licensed stock photos used until the client provides real ones)
- Legal review of policies (drafts provided; the client's attorney approves)
- Ongoing maintenance after launch (available separately)

## Client responsibilities
- Business details: name, address, phone, hours, license numbers, doctor bio, photos
- Medical accuracy review of all health content
- **HIPAA compliance, telehealth rules, and all prescribing decisions.** The prescription request form
  is provided as requested by the client.
- Accounts registered in the client's name (domain, hosting, database, email)
